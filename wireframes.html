Wireframes & user flow

Low-fidelity wireframes for the music app empty.string. Grey boxes are content areas, crossed boxes are cover art, red dots are annotations. The real, playable prototype lives at femboyss.github.io/empty-strings. These frames show structure only, no colors or real content.

01

Mobile screens

Four tabs at the bottom (Home, Search, Radio, Library). The mini player sits above the tab bar on every screen once something plays, so music never stops while you navigate.

02

Sheets & overlays

Secondary actions open as bottom sheets on the phone (thumb-friendly, swipe down to close) and as centered dialogs on desktop.

03

Desktop layout (window 900px and wider)

Same screens, website layout: the tab bar becomes a sidebar, the mini player becomes a full-width player bar, card rows become grids and sheets become centered dialogs. Smaller desktop windows show a scaled phone preview instead.

06

Design decisions

No account, no key

Library, playlists and history are stored on the device, so the app works instantly for anyone who opens the link.

Thumb zone first

Tabs, mini player and sheets are at the bottom where the thumb reaches on a phone. Primary actions use the accent color only.

One collection template

Albums, playlists, artists, charts and Archive releases all share one layout, so users learn it once.

Live is visibly different

Radio shows a red LIVE marker instead of a duration and a live bar instead of a seek bar, because you cannot seek in a broadcast.

Honest states

Skeleton loaders while loading, clear empty and error messages with a retry button, and a Sources page that shows which API is reachable.

Accessible

Keyboard shortcuts, visible focus rings, labels on every icon button, and reduced motion when the system asks for it.
